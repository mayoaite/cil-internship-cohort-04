# PESTEL Analysis of crypto market in Nigeria

## Political
The Nigerian government has seen cryptocurrency as a threat to traditional currency because it is decentralized. The government enacted a law banning the sale or purchase of any cryptocurrency in the country. As such, people have ventured into P2P trading to buy or sell crypto thereby increasing the purchase price.
## Economic
A lot of young Nigerians make money from arbitrage off the crypto market. Due to the bottleneck that comes with acquiring the dollar in Nigeria, people now resort to crypto to make most of their dealings leading to a surge in crypto transactions. 
## Sociological
The demography of the country has a huge impact on the crypto market since it has to do with technology. It means youths rather than adults of ages 50 or older drift towards the market. 
## Technological
Technologically savvy people tend towards crypto and with the advent of Web3, it makes blockchain technology easier to navigate. 
## Environment
This may not have a direct bearing on the market
## Legal
Due to the restriction placed on the crypto market by the Nigerian government, there are legal implications for those caught trading crypto in Nigeria and the banks and tech companies are well aware of this.
