# Stakeholders
1.	**Office staff:** They will add customers to the system, revise order details, print invoices, and/or email them to customers.
2.	**Customers:** Being end users, they can enroll themselves via the company’s website.
3.	**Managing Director:** As a decision maker will be seeing weekly unpaid invoices and also hope to reconcile them with the company’s payroll system.
4.	**Production Manager:** In a support capacity to view all customer orders to plan production.
5.	**Office Supervisor:** As an information provider will provide information on how all of the company’s activities from the office onsite, where she and her two office staff process customer orders and invoices.

# Requirement Elicitation Techniques
-	**Brainstorming** with the office staff to know what is to be included.
-	**Survey** by issuing questionnaires to the customers to understand what they expect to be included in the product.
-	**Observation** as a means to understand how the company’s activities and processes via the Office Supervisor.
