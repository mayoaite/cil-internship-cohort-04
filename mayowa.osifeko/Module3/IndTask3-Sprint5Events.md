**Overview**

As a Business Analyst working for a product development company. I have been selected as part of a team to work on a new project. Here is how I would leverage my business analysis skills in each scrum event.

**The Sprint**

I will focus on refining and prioritizing the product backlog items and identify which ones will be in the next sprint. I will also support the Product Owner (PO) or their proxy as they must be available and accessible during the sprint to answer questions from team members and “inspect” their work to facilitate adaptability.

**Sprint planning**

I will mentor, coach, and support the PO in ensuring the product backlog items (PBIs) for a particular sprint are as “ready” (refined) as possible. I will also make sure each PBI meets the INVEST criteria (independent, negotiable, valuable, estimable, small, and testable), and ensure it includes robust acceptance criteria. If necessary, I can help model the PBI and/or acceptance criteria if it helps the team understand what is needed. 
I will then confirm an understanding of in-scope PBIs in support of (re)sizing efforts, and identifying, and estimating tasks by the Development Team. Being the BA, I will facilitate the Sprint Planning meeting.

**Daily Scrums**

As a member of the development team, I must participate in daily scrums. I will ensure there is active engagement in the event and help elicit relevant information from other team members. In the absence of the Scrum Master, I will help facilitate the daily Scrum.

**Sprint Review**

As a business analyst, I can assist the Product Owner in preparing for the Sprint Review by identifying business stakeholders who may want to attend, as well as implementing the logistics for the Review (e.g., invitations, physical or virtual room reservation/set-up, snacks, etc.).

I can determine which facilitation or presentation techniques to use and/or elicit feedback regarding demonstrated functionality. I will also work with the team after the Sprint Review to incorporate feedback into the Product Backlog and upcoming Sprint Planning meeting.

**Sprint Retrospective**

As a business analyst, I will participate and also be a role model for active engagement. I may facilitate the event, plan the agenda, determine facilitation techniques to use for the Retrospective. I can also obtain needed facilitation materials, and document the agreed practices (things that will be done differently) to apply to the next Sprint.
