## Overview
Into The Wild, a company that has been running for five years offering outward-bound experiences for both children and adults is losing customers because they are unable to contact customers in a timely manner and cannot manage bookings on a mobile device. Being the BA consultant, I have highlighted some features of an app that will be built to help solve this problem.
